Delete your MintooSuite account and data

App: MintooSuite (com.mintoosuite.terminal). Developer: Muhammad Awais, Islamabad, Pakistan. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open MintooSuite.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap "Delete my account and data", then tap again to confirm.

This erases all data stored on your phone by the app: settings, practice trades, saved notes, Baba AI chat and API keys. The app then restarts clean.

Option 2: by email

If you cannot open the app, email awaisrajpoot2@yahoo.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MintooSuite account". We will delete your online sign-in record (email address and password check kept by Google Firebase Authentication) within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your online account (email and sign-in record) and everything the app stored on your phone.
  • Not held by us: exchange API keys, wallet words and trade history. They live only on your phone, so they are removed when you use Option 1 or uninstall the app.
  • Not controlled by us: data held by outside services you used, such as your exchange or Google. Ask them directly. Google ad data is handled under Google's own policy.
  • Kept: nothing about you, except a short note that a deletion was requested, if the law requires it.
