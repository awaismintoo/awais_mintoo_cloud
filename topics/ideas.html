Questions

Search bar in Ideas: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Under the Ideas / Scanner / New Plan tabs there is a search bar. It says if the coin is in Ideas; if not, it offers Gainers/Losers/Volume, then a whole-app search (MEXC, Bitget). Ideas now show one row per coin, and rows the live bar does not confirm are moved to the bottom with a label.

Build 382: smarter entry check (LONG and SHORT) and fairer ideas: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Before the app shows LONG or SHORT, every coin now passes one shared Entry check: it blocks a LONG on a coin that already pumped (about 15% or more today) or whose 15-minute trend has turned down or is making lower highs, and the same for SHORT on a coin that already crashed. A falling coin can now be a SHORT when its trend agrees, and a rising coin a LONG. Needs at least 3 of 5 confirmations (price not stretched, trend strength, momentum, volume, 1-hour trend). The reason is written on the bar, for example "Entry check: the 15-minute trend points down". Best ideas used to need a chance the math could never reach, so almost nothing qualified; the bar is now fair for the stop and target used. The same check works for Bitget, MEXC, OKX and the coins of the other exchanges too. Bot Doctor also uses this check and only gives side/hour advice after 30 trades. It reduces bad entries; it cannot guarantee profit or beat any other tool every time.

Ideas opens on "Do now": how does it work in MintooSuite?
Ideas now opens on the Do now tab. At the top are the deals that pass every check this minute, each with a timer. Below is a list of the ideas the app does have that are still waiting, with the one check each is stuck on (for example "Entry check: the 15-minute trend points up" or "the chart bar says WAIT"). Waiting ideas are not entries. If the 15-minute chart looks clear but Baba Chief says WAIT, open the adviser buttons: usually the 1-hour, 4-hour and daily charts (Time-frame scan) still point the other way, which makes a trade against the bigger trend riskier. The bar no longer shows LONG or SHORT first and WAIT seconds later: it stays on WAIT (checking) until the entry check and the chart signal are both read, then shows the final answer.

Live entry check (build 384): how does it work in MintooSuite?
On top of the 15-minute read, the entry check now watches the live price every second: a LONG is not recommended once the price is below the 15-minute trend stop or has dropped more than 2.5 average candles since the read, and a SHORT is not recommended once the price is above the trend stop or has jumped by the same amount. The reason is written on the bar. The old UT Bot line on the chart only changes when a candle closes, so it can still say BUY side for a moment after a fast drop; check the Chief bar first, it now checks live.

Dual criteria: 15-minute rule + FAST realtime lane (build 384): how does it work in MintooSuite?
The entry check now has two lanes. 15-minute confirmed: the 15-minute and 1-hour trends agree (the usual thumb rule). FAST realtime lane: for pumps, small coins and big coins that just started to move, a LONG or SHORT can show before the 15-minute candle agrees when the 1-minute trend, a real 15-minute-size move, rising volume and momentum all agree, the last candle is not a spike and price is not stretched. Those calls are labelled FAST (never above 25% moved today, and a pump is allowed only when most of the move is fresh) and need a small size, because they are riskier. The FAST lane also cancels a call at once when the 1-minute trend breaks the other way with a real move. Data refreshes about every 15 seconds on the open chart. Baba experience veto and the too-late rule still apply. No guarantee: fast moves can reverse in seconds.

Build 385: Baba pick target/stop and Do now matches the chart: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Most Baba pick ideas used a target only half the size of the stop. That needs a win rate above about 70% just to break even after fees, so almost every idea showed "after fees the odds are not better than break-even". The plan now uses a stop of 1.5 average candles and a target of 1.8 times the stop (the same shape as the Analyst plan), which needs roughly 45 to 50% wins to break even after fees. Do now also now runs the exact same chart signal check as the chart bar, so the list and the chart usually agree; they can still differ for a few seconds when the price moves. The reason is written on the line.
