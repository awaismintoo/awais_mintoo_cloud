Partners and referral links (build 492): how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open More > Pakistan > Partners. Paste your Exness partner link or Binance, Bybit, OKX referral links (https only). People open a licensed broker from your link and the broker holds their money and trades; you earn the broker's commission. The app itself cannot hold money or place customers' orders without broker licences. The window also keeps your own directory of money exchangers and brokers and a checklist for an SBP-licensed exchanger or a PSX/SECP broker.
