📿 Hadith (Urdu): daily hadith, offline and online

آج کی حدیث، آف لائن 361 احادیث اور آن لائن کتب احادیث، اردو ترجمہ اور حوالہ. Free in the MintooSuite app, Pakistan.

About

Each time you open the app today's hadith appears (choose every open, every 3 hours or once a day in the Hadith window) at the top with its Urdu translation and reference (book and hadith number). Swipe it sideways or wait 2 minutes. The small language buttons on it switch the hadith to Arabic, English, Turkish, Indonesian, Bangla, French, Russian or Tamil at once (Urdu works offline; another language needs internet once and then stays saved), and 🌐 Other opens Google Translate for any language; your choice is remembered. Tap "پوری پڑھیں" to read it fully. More > Islam > Hadith has 361 hadiths offline (search by word or number, save favourites, listen in Urdu) and online reading of Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah and Muwatta Malik with Arabic. The popup can be switched off there.

Questions

Hadith in Urdu: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Every time the app opens (you can choose every open, every 3 hours or once a day in More > Islam > Hadith), today's hadith (Urdu translation with reference) shows at the top; swipe it away or it goes after 2 minutes. Small language buttons on it show it in Arabic, English, Turkish, Indonesian, Bangla, French, Russian, Tamil, or any language through Google Translate. More > Islam > Hadith (Urdu): today's hadith, 361 hadiths from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim offline, and online reading of seven hadith books in Urdu with Arabic. For religious rulings ask a qualified scholar.

Complete hadith books offline: how does it work in MintooSuite?
More > Hadith: 📥 Complete hadith books: download Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah and Muwatta Malik (Urdu) once; then read and search them offline. The daily hadith then rotates through all downloaded books.
