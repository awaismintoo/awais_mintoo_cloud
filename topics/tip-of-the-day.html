What you find inside

  • TIP OF THE DAY
  • Habit
  • Trade less
  • A new tip every day
  • Risk
  • Decide the most you can lose on a trade BEFORE you enter
  • A stop-loss is not optional
  • Leverage multiplies mistakes as much as profits
  • Use isolated margin so one bad trade cannot take your whole futures balance.
  • Never trade money you need for rent, food, school fees or family
  • Do not average down on a losing trade
  • Set a daily loss limit (for example 3%)
  • Fees and slippage are real
  • Do not open many trades in the same direction
  • Keep a journal
  • Paper trade a new idea for at least 50 trades before using real money
  • Write your rules down once, then follow them
  • After a big win or a big loss, take a break. Emotions are loudest right after an outcome.
  • Review your week every Sunday: what worked, what broke a rule, one thing to improve.
  • Do not trade when tired, angry, hungry or in a hurry. Sleep protects your account.
  • Compare yourself with yesterday's you, not with screenshots of other people's wins.
  • Market
  • The trend on the 1-hour and 4-hour charts matters more than a 1-minute wiggle
  • When Bitcoin moves sharply, most coins follow. Check Bitcoin before trading an altcoin.
  • A coin up 20% or more today is often late. Chasing is the most common beginner loss.
  • Volume confirms moves. A breakout on thin volume often fails.
  • Weekends and late hours have thinner liquidity, so spreads and spikes are worse.
  • Funding rate extremes on futures show a crowded side
  • Support and resistance are zones, not exact lines. Give your stop room beyond the zone.
  • RSI above 70 or below 30 is a warning of stretch, not an automatic sell or buy.
  • Sideways markets punish trend strategies
  • Safety
  • Create exchange API keys with Withdrawal OFF and trusted IPs only
  • Turn on Google Authenticator or a passkey on every exchange. Avoid SMS-only security.
  • Type exchange addresses yourself or use your saved bookmark
  • Write your wallet's 12 words on paper and keep it offline
  • Test withdrawals and transfers with a tiny amount first, on the correct network.
  • Use the Trade card under a chart
  • Baba Practice Bot's Smart Guard shows why it skipped a trade
  • The real bot is locked until you type UNLOCK, and the STOP button stops all bots
  • Use Menu > Problem log if anything freezes. It records errors and helps fix them quickly.
  • Check Bot Doctor weekly: it explains why trades lost and what to change.
  • Set your alerts for coins you follow so you do not stare at the screen all day.
  • Try Calm screen in Look & mode if you want a quieter, battery-friendly app.
  • Mindset
