Questions

Buy, sell, rent, hire in the app (build 491): how does it work in MintooSuite?
Menu > Buy & sell is a listings board: sellers post pictures, price, city and phone, with the type Sale, Rent, Hire (service or labour) or Instalments (hire purchase); the owner approves each post first. The app does not take payments or deliver goods, so the deal happens between the two people. Safe steps: 1) meet in a public place or at the shop; 2) check the item or the papers (registry, fard, receipts) yourself; 3) pay only against handover and get a written receipt; 4) for business, property or instalments write the agreement and use a lawyer; 5) never send advance money to a stranger. The owner must set up Firebase once in Buy & sell > Setup for listings to appear.

Videos inside the app (build 492): how does it work in MintooSuite?
YouTube and Vimeo links in Buy and sell listings and in your saved places play inside the app after you tap Play. They need internet. Other video links open in your browser.

Listings last 60 days (build 492): how does it work in MintooSuite?
Buy and sell listings, their pictures (up to 3, small), video links and reviews disappear after 60 days and are deleted automatically when you or the owner next open the app. Post again to renew. Pictures are stored inside the listing, so no Firebase Storage is needed.

Your ads everywhere: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Buy & sell > Sell asks the full details buyers want (size, rooms, make, model, year, km, PTA…). My ads keeps your MintooSuite ads and the links of your ads on OLX, PakWheels, Zameen, Graana and others in one place, and "Post the same ad on other sites" copies your ad text and opens each site to paste it. Those sites do not allow other apps to sign in and post for you.

Reports, blocking and your data: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Report a listing or review with 🚩 and block a seller or reviewer with 🚫 in Buy & sell. Report an AI picture in Photo studio. Profile > Delete my account and data removes everything on your phone plus your listings, reviews and shared price notes online. Privacy policy: Menu > Legal.

Buy & sell owner Setup and Firebase rules: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open Buy & sell, scroll to the very bottom and tap the small grey "Owner? Tap here": the ⚙️ Setup tab opens at the top. Tap Save and test (your owner ID appears), then 📋 Copy Firestore rules: your ID is already inside. In console.firebase.google.com open your project > Firestore Database > Rules, select all, paste, Publish. Then 📋 Copy Storage rules > Storage > Rules > paste > Publish.
