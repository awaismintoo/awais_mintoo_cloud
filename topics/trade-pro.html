Questions

Build 381: notifications open the coin chart; Binance positions refresh faster: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Tap a notification (Baba pick, pump alert, price alert) and the app opens that coin chart. This needs the new installer and an app update. Positions you open on Binance now show in Trade windows (Trade Pro and the Trading Terminal) within a few seconds: the app re-checks every 4 seconds while one of them is open and straight away when you come back to the app. It needs your read-only API key saved under Profile > Connections (keep Withdraw and Transfer permissions OFF). The check uses Binance public account calls, so it can be a few seconds behind the exchange.

Chart bar now agrees with Bots tab (build 405): how does it work in MintooSuite?
If a bot signal is listed in Book-rule trades, the chart bar of that coin now shows the same Long/Short instead of WAIT (it used to read an older copy of the decision). Coins that already moved more than 30% today are no longer offered as bot signals in either direction: the move is too wild and spikes can hit any stop.

Lite mode and calm trading (build 406): how does it work in MintooSuite?
The bot scanner now stops while Trade Pro is open, so placing an order has the whole phone to itself; signals already found stay held for their 15 minutes. If Android has closed the app twice in six hours, the app switches itself to Lite: it checks the 120 busiest coins, 4 per step instead of 14, and keeps less chart data in memory. For best results set Android Settings > Apps > MintooSuite > Battery to Unrestricted.

Slimmer Trade Pro bottom bar (build 483): how does it work in MintooSuite?
The Long / Short / Trade now bar with Recommended and Linked masters is now two slim rows (it was three tall ones) and the page has extra room at the bottom so nothing is hidden behind it.
