About

More > Life and shopping > City life. 📍 Area: search any city or area like a weather app, or tap Use my location; saved areas sit in a row to switch fast. You see live weather, 24 hours, 16 days, air quality, sunrise and sunset, prayer times and Baba's advice; it updates by itself every 10-15 minutes and stays saved with its date and time offline. 🍽 Food: pick a dish and compare places by price, discount and taste from real price notes. 🎉 Events: national and Islamic days, yearly shows (with a Check dates button), picnic and tourist spots with map and video, your own events. 📅 Calendar: Gregorian with Hijri, public holidays (Pakistan built in, other countries online), weather on each day. Each has "Show at the bottom of Home" and "Add widget to phone home screen".

Questions

Weather, air and prayer times: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open City life > 📍 Area. Search your area (or Use my location): live weather, next 24 hours, 16 days, air quality with advice, sunrise/sunset and prayer times. It updates by itself and stays saved offline with its time. You can show it at the bottom of Home and as a phone widget.

Holidays and Hijri calendar: how does it work in MintooSuite?
City life > 📅 Calendar shows every date with its Hijri date, public holidays (Pakistan 2026 from the Cabinet Division notifications; Islamic days of other years are expected and depend on the moon) and the weather for the next 16 days. Other countries load online.

Events and places to visit: how does it work in MintooSuite?
City life > 🎉 Events: coming national and Islamic days, yearly shows and festivals with a Check dates button, picnic and tourist spots (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Murree, Hunza, Swat and more) with map and video, nearby attractions from OpenStreetMap, and your own events. Live listings open from the links.

Compare food prices: how does it work in MintooSuite?
City life > 🍽 Food: pick a dish to see who sells it cheapest, best taste and best value near you, from real price notes with dates. Add the price you paid to help others. Links: restaurants near you, foodpanda, bank card deals, video reviews.

Weather and forecast: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Home (bottom) shows your area's weather; tap Full forecast for the hourly forecast, 9 days, air quality, sunrise, prayer times and advice. Change or search any area (like a weather app) in City life › Weather and area.

Events near you: how does it work in MintooSuite?
City life › Events › Live events and shows: expos, exhibitions, car shows, concerts and festivals for Islamabad and 11 more cities, Today / This week / This month, saved offline, with More details, pictures, videos and map.
