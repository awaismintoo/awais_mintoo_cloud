Questions

Build 458: Photo studio and Marketplace window: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Photo studio (Menu > Pakistan): pick a photo and tap Beard, Long hair, Older, Younger, Glasses, Smile, Bald or Clean background, or type your own edit. It uses OpenAI image editing with your own key (typed in the window, stored on this phone only), so it needs internet and costs a few cents per edit. Marketplace (Menu > Pakistan): shows listings from a feed address you set (format in the PREREQUISITES folder), searchable by category and city, last copy works offline; each card opens the seller's own buy page. Live listings need seller agreements and a feed that you set up.

AI interior design: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open Photo studio, pick a photo of your room, then tap a room style (Luxury modern, Minimal, Pakistani traditional, new wall colour, new sofa, false ceiling, bedroom or kitchen makeover) or type exactly what you want in "your own change". The AI keeps the walls, windows and doors and redesigns the rest. Needs internet and your own OpenAI key (a few cents per picture).
