Music and movies: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open Music & movies: top songs by country, search songs and movies by name and year with thumbnails and 30-second previews. Free & legal downloads (public-domain films and Creative-Commons music, with file sizes) are in the Free & legal tab. Copyrighted songs and films are not offered as free downloads; play them on YouTube, Spotify or a streaming app. Background music can be turned on there.
