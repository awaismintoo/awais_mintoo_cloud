🥬 Grocery: list, prices, order online

Shopping list, compare shop prices and discounts, order online from real stores, stores near you. Free in the MintooSuite app, Pakistan.

About

Grocery > Prices, Fruits & vegetables and Rates fill in automatically every week from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (17 cities), for your nearest city, saved offline with the date, with where to buy near you. Typing a price is optional.

Grocery has two new tabs. Fruits & veg: pictures, season, in-season badge, how to pick fresh and store, videos, add to list, prices near you and where to buy. Rates: the official weekly average of 51 essential items from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (17 cities, updated automatically, saved offline with date and time), DC (district) rate lists with links and your saved DC rates, and local market prices near your area. Home also shows a Word of the day from the PK glossary. Weather now comes from MET Norway for the nearest of 52 cities, refreshed every hour.
