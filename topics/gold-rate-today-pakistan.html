Buying gold safely

  • Ask for the karat (24K, 22K or 21K) in writing on the bill.
  • Check the weight on the shop's scale in grams and tolas.
  • Making charges (mazdoori) and wastage (kaat) are added on jewellery; compare them between shops.
  • Keep the bill: it decides the resale price.
