Safety checks before a trade: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Before opening a trade the app refuses a price older than 30 seconds, ignores an accidental double tap (1.5 s), warns when the price moved more than a quarter of the idea’s own stop distance (and offers “Use today’s price”), and refuses a practice trade whose stop sits beyond the liquidation price. Practice stop-losses fill at the real price after a gap, so a gap can lose more than planned, as in real trading.
