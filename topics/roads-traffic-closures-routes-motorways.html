Questions

Roads & traffic: how does it work in MintooSuite?
More › Life and shopping › 🚦 Roads & traffic: 📢 Notices shows road closures, container blockades, diversions and motorway status for Islamabad, Rawalpindi, motorways, Lahore, Karachi and Peshawar from the last 2 days (updates every hour, saved offline). Tap ⛔ Mark on map to put a closed spot on the map. 🗺️ Map & route: type where you want to go; the app shows up to 3 driving routes, marks blocked spots in red and the best route that avoids them in green; map pieces you have seen and your last route work offline. Voice guidance speaks turn-by-turn directions with your GPS in English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Persian or Chinese (non-English needs the language pack once). Help lines: NHMP 130, Islamabad Traffic Police 1915, Rescue 1122.

Maps in MintooSuite: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Every 📍 Map in the app opens the MintooSuite map first (OpenStreetMap; map pieces you have seen are kept offline), with the place marked and buttons for Google Maps or an offline map app. For a complete offline map of all Pakistan install Organic Maps (free) and download Pakistan inside it once; then "Open in offline map app" works with no internet. Roads & traffic draws routes around blocked roads.
