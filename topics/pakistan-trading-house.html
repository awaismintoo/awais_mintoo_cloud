Questions

Professional advisor boxes in the Pakistan desk (build 487): how does it work in MintooSuite?
In the Pakistan Trading House, the advisor buttons (What looks strong, Explain this sector, Monthly plan, Avoid scams) now open a Professional advisor box right there, with a small arrow to fold it. Each PSX tab (Hot, Autos, Sectors, Real estate, All shares, More) opens its own advisor note when you tap it. Shop and property buttons answer in a box at the top instead of the chat when the app has the answer built in; Brokers and Local and offers have their own box. These are explanations and plans, never tips or promises of profit.

Offline and last updated: how does it work in MintooSuite?
Data windows show a small Last updated line (date and time) at the top right, with a 🔄 to update now. Offline they show the saved copy with its date and time, and update by themselves when you are back online.

PSX prices offline: how does it work in MintooSuite?
PSX share prices, the KSE-100 and the company list are saved on your phone each time they load and are shown offline with their date and time (orange line at the top of the PSX window). If the PSX site does not answer and nothing was saved yet, the window says so and offers Try again and the official PSX Data Portal.
