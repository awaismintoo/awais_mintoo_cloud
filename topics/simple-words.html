What you find inside

  • 📘 Simple words
  • 33 trading words in plain language, each with an example and Roman Urdu
  • Basics
  • Risk
  • Reading charts
  • Costs
  • Leverage
  • Borrowed power. It makes wins and losses bigger.
  • Margin
  • The money locked as a deposit for one trade.
  • Stop-loss
  • An automatic exit that limits how much you can lose.
  • Take-profit
  • An automatic exit that locks in a gain.
  • Long / Buy
  • You win if the price goes UP.
  • Short / Sell
  • You win if the price goes DOWN.
  • Liquidation
  • The exchange closes your trade because the margin is used up.
  • Funding fee
  • A small fee paid or received every 8 hours for holding a futures trade.
  • How much the price usually moves. Bigger means wilder.
  • Spread and slippage
  • The small extra cost of buying and selling.
  • Spot and Futures
  • Spot: you own the coin. Futures: you bet on price with leverage.
  • Paper trading
  • Practice with pretend money. Nothing real is risked.
  • Pump
  • A very fast price jump, often on small or new coins.
  • A 0-to-100 gauge of how fast price has moved lately.
  • A smoothed average price that follows recent candles more closely.
  • UT Bot and Supertrend
  • Two popular rules that flip between BUY and SELL as price moves.
  • Candle
  • One bar on the chart showing open, close, high and low for a period.
  • Volume
  • How much was traded in a period.
  • Support and resistance
  • Price levels where the market has often paused or turned.
  • Trend
  • The general direction of price over a stretch of time.
  • Risk and reward
  • How much you risk compared with how much you could gain.
