How to trade PSX shares: how does it work in MintooSuite?
This app cannot place PSX orders: they go only through a licensed PSX broker. Steps: 1) pick a licensed, SECP-registered broker (Pakistan Trading House > Brokers has a checklist); 2) open the account with CNIC, own bank account, photo and signature; 3) a CDC sub-account or investor account holds your shares; 4) send money only to the broker company account; 5) place Limit orders in the broker app Monday to Friday, settlement T+2; 6) ask for fees and tax details in writing; 7) start small and ignore guaranteed-profit tips. Not personal financial advice.
