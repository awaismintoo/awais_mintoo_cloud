Land units: how does it work in MintooSuite?
1 marla = 272.25 sq ft (revenue; schemes may use 250 or 225). 1 kanal = 20 marla = 5,445 sq ft. 1 bigha (Punjab) = 4 kanal. 1 killa (qila) = 1 acre = 8 kanal = 43,560 sq ft. 1 murabba = 25 acres. 1 jarib (Sindh) = half an acre. 1 hectare = 2.471 acres.
