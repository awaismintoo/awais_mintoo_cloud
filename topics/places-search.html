Places search (build 492): how does it work in MintooSuite?
Open More > Pakistan > Places. Type an area (Hostel City Park Road Islamabad, Quetta, any city in the world), pick a type or type a shop name, and tap Search. Results come from OpenStreetMap with map, call and WhatsApp buttons. Tap Save offline to keep a place without internet, then add your stars, notes, a picture and a YouTube link. Saved items are removed after 60 days unless you open them. OpenStreetMap has no ratings, so Reviews and Videos open Google Maps and YouTube.
