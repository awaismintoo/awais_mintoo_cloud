MintooSuite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

MintooSuite works mostly on your phone. We do not sell your data and we do not collect your name, ID or exchange passwords on our own servers. Some optional features use outside services, listed below. Last updated 30 October 2026.

What the app stores on your device

Your settings (language, theme, zoom, alerts), the ideas and practice (pretend-money) trades the app showed you, saved Wikipedia notes and your Baba AI chat. Exchange API keys are kept on the phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore when the phone supports it, and are sent only to the exchange you chose. The 12 wallet words stay on the phone only. We cannot read or recover them.

Optional online account

If you create an online account, your email address and a password check are stored by Google Firebase Authentication so you can sign in again. You can ask us to delete the account by email.

Ads and analytics

The free version may show banner ads from Google AdMob on safe screens (never on trading, wallet or chart screens). Google may use the advertising ID for this, and shows its own consent screen where the law requires it. Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) are off unless you switch them on. You can change this in the app.

Network requests the app makes

Live prices, news and market data come from public services such as Binance, MEXC, Bitget, Bybit, OKX, CoinGecko, Google News, Wikipedia and open-meteo (weather). Those services see your phone's internet address like any website would. If you use Baba AI online and agree to it, the text you type is sent to a free third-party AI service to get an answer. Do not type secrets, passwords or ID numbers into it.

Permissions and why

Notifications and background service: price alerts and bots you switch on. Battery optimisation: so alerts keep working. Display over other apps: floating alerts, only if you allow it. Location: optional, for local markets, offers and the world clock. Microphone: only when you press the mic. Camera and photos/files: only to attach a picture or file you choose. You can turn any of them off in Android settings.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile, then "Delete my account and data", and tap to confirm. This wipes everything stored on your phone. You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall it. To delete your online account (email and sign-in record) without the app, see how to delete your account or email awaisrajpoot2@yahoo.com.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local alerts generated on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

No financial advice

The app shows market information and rule-based ideas. It is not financial advice, nothing it shows is guaranteed, and trading can lose money. The app places a real order only if you have added your own Binance API keys, and only after you review and confirm that order. Leveraged trading can lose your whole margin quickly. You are responsible for every order. Never give an API key withdrawal permission.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
