MintooSuite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 (app build 2026-11-12.495)

Summary

MintooSuite works mostly on your phone. We do not sell your data. We do not run our own servers for your personal data; a few optional features use outside services, listed below. You can delete your data at any time (see "Deleting your data").

What stays on your phone

Settings (language, theme, zoom, alerts), practice (pretend-money) trades, saved notes, your Baba AI chat, saved areas and weather copies, your shopping list, price notes, saved songs and movies, calculator history, your own events, and copies of data shown offline. Exchange API keys and your own AI key are kept on the phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore where the phone supports it, and are sent only to the service they belong to. The 12 wallet words never leave the phone; we cannot read or recover them. A WireGuard VPN configuration you import stays on the phone.

Optional online account

If you create an online account, your email address and a password check are stored by Google Firebase Authentication so you can sign in again and keep backups.

Buy & sell listings and reviews

If you post a listing, the details you type (title, price, city, description, the details you choose to add, your name if you add it, your phone or WhatsApp number), up to 3 small pictures and an optional video link are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under an anonymous app ID, are checked by the app owner, and are then shown to everyone who uses the app. Reviews you post are public. Listings, pictures and reviews are deleted automatically after 60 days; you can delete yours earlier in My ads. Do not post CNIC numbers or private documents. You can report a listing in the app.

Shared price notes

If you tick "Share this price with everyone", the item, place name, area, price, discount, your quality stars and an approximate location (about 100 m) are stored in Firebase without your name or phone and shown to other users for 60 days.

Location

Optional. If you tap "Use my location", your approximate position is used on the phone and sent, without your name, to the weather service (Open-Meteo), the prayer-time service (AlAdhan), and OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Overpass) to get the weather, prayer times, the area name and places nearby. Searching an area by name sends that name to the same services.

AI features

Baba AI answers most questions from knowledge built into the app. If you switch on online AI, the text you type is sent to the AI service you chose. Photo studio sends the photo you pick and your instruction to OpenAI using your own OpenAI key, only when you tap an edit; do not use photos of other people without their permission. You can report an AI answer or picture in the app.

Voice

When you press the microphone, your phone's own speech service (for example Google speech services) turns your voice into text. Read-aloud uses the phone's own text-to-speech.

Error reports

Only if you agree to "Help improve the app": a short cleaned error message, the app version and the screen name may be sent so it can be fixed. It never includes your name, email, wallet, balances, keys or trades.

Permissions and why

Notifications and background service: alerts and bots you switch on. Location: optional, for weather, prayer times and places near you. Microphone: only while you press the mic. Camera and photos: only to attach or edit a picture you choose. VPN: only if you import your own VPN configuration and tap Connect. Home-screen widget: shows the last weather, date and event the app saved. You can turn permissions off in Android settings.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile, then "Delete my account and data". This wipes everything on your phone and, with your password, your online account, cloud backups and device list. Your listings can be deleted in Buy & sell > My ads, and are removed automatically after 60 days. Without the app: see how to delete your account or email awaisrajpoot2@yahoo.com.

No financial advice

The app shows market information and rule-based ideas. It is not financial advice, nothing is guaranteed, and trading can lose money. Real orders are placed only with your own API keys, after you review and confirm them. Never give an API key withdrawal permission.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
