MintooSuite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

MintooSuite has no account or login of its own, no ads and no analytics. We collect no personal information. If you choose to use the optional Binance trading feature, your own Binance API keys stay on your device and are sent only to Binance. Your settings and the virtual (paper) trading account are stored only on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

Your language, theme, leverage choice, notification settings, a log of the ideas the app showed you, the virtual paper-trading account, and, only if you add them, your Binance API key and secret. These are kept in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent to us. You can erase all of it by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings.

Network requests the app makes

To show live prices and news, the app contacts public services: Binance public market data (binance.com), and public news feeds (CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, BBC, Google News) through relay services (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io, codetabs.com, rss2json.com). Those services can see your device's IP address in the normal way when you connect to them. The app sends them no personal information. If you add Binance API keys, the app also sends signed requests directly from your device to Binance (fapi.binance.com, or the Binance testnet in Test mode) to check your balance and to place or close the orders you confirm. We never receive your keys or your orders. The app does not connect to Exness.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local alerts generated on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

No financial advice

The app shows market information and rule-based ideas. It is not financial advice, nothing it shows is guaranteed, and trading can lose money. The app places a real order only if you have added your own Binance API keys, and only after you review and confirm that order. Leveraged trading can lose your whole margin quickly. You are responsible for every order. Never give an API key withdrawal permission.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
