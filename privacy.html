MintooSuite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Summary

MintooSuite has no account, no login, no ads and no analytics. It collects no personal information. Your settings and the virtual (paper) trading account are stored only on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

Your language, theme, leverage choice, notification settings, a log of the ideas the app showed you, and the virtual paper-trading account. You can erase all of it by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings.

Network requests the app makes

To show live prices and news, the app contacts public services: Binance public market data (binance.com), and public news feeds (CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, BBC, Google News) through relay services (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io, codetabs.com, rss2json.com). Those services can see your device's IP address in the normal way when you connect to them. The app sends them no personal information and does not log in to any account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local alerts generated on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

No financial advice

The app shows market information and rule-based ideas. It is not financial advice, nothing it shows is guaranteed, and trading can lose money. The app places no real trades.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
